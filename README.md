This data comprises four years of tracking and sampling of masked boobies (Sula dactylatra) in Fernando de Noronha Archipelago, Brazil, including GPS data and stable isotopes data (carbon an nitrogen)
from blood of breeding birds and muscle of regurgitated prey. 
More information about methods can be accessed in the published article "Flexible foraging strategies of a tropical seabird in the western Atlantic Ocean",
Journal of Experimental Marine Biology and Ecology (in press)
